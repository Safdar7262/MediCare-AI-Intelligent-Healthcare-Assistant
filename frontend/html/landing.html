How booking works

Three steps from “I need to see someone” to a confirmed appointment.

  1. Step 1

    Find a doctor

    Search by name or specialty and compare experience, fees and available days.

  2. Step 2

    Pick a date and time

    Choose from the doctor’s working days and hours, add a note about your symptoms, and review.

  3. Step 3

    Get confirmation

    The doctor confirms your request and you are notified in the app. Reschedule or cancel from your appointments.

Find a doctor

An assistant that knows its limits

The AI assistant helps with general health information and with using MediCare. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medicine, or replace a doctor.

If you describe symptoms that could be an emergency, it tells you to call your local emergency number straight away.

Emergency? Call 112 or go to the nearest hospital. Do not wait for a reply from the assistant.
Avoid sharing more personal details than you need to. Your conversations are visible only to your account.

Questions people ask

How do I book an appointment?

Log in, open Find a doctor, choose a doctor, then pick a date and time. You will see the request under Appointments straight away, marked Pending until the doctor confirms.

Can the AI assistant replace a doctor?

No. It offers general information and helps you find the right specialist. It cannot diagnose or prescribe. Always consult a licensed doctor for medical decisions.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Open Appointments, pick the visit, and choose Reschedule or Cancel. Please make changes as early as you can so the slot can be offered to someone else.

Is my personal and medical data safe?

Your account is protected by authentication, and what you can see depends on your role. Details are used to manage your appointments and records.

What should I do in a medical emergency?

Do not use this platform for emergencies. Call your local emergency number (112 in India) or go to the nearest hospital immediately.

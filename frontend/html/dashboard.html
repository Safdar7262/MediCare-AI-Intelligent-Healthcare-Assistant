Quick answers

How do I book an appointment?

Open Find a doctor, choose a doctor, then pick a date and time. Your request shows under Appointments as Pending until the doctor confirms.

Can the AI assistant replace a doctor?

No. It gives general information and can’t diagnose or prescribe. Always consult a licensed doctor for medical decisions.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Go to Appointments, find the visit and choose Reschedule or Cancel. Please change plans as early as you can.

What if it’s an emergency?

Don’t use this platform for emergencies. Call 112 in India or go to the nearest hospital immediately.
